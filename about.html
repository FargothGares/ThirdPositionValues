About ThirdPositionValues

What is ThirdPositionValues?

ThirdPositionValues is a test that aims to classify political beliefs of people who identify with the Third Political Position, referring to a broad range of movements which are anti-capitalist, anti-Marxist and nationalist. The test assumes you are a nationalist. If you aren't a nationalist, or are a liberal or a Marxist, you will certainly find questions that won't make sense for you.

What are the 16 values?

There are 8 independent axes - Practical, Class, Diplomatic, Identity, Cultural, Technological, Political and Religious - and each has two opposing values assigned to them:

Practical Axis

Insurrection

measures how willing you are to employ illegal and/or violent tactics to take power. Those who lean towards Insurrection typically view the status quo as illegitimate or otherwise unworthy of respect, and don't view working within the system as a path to power.



Reform

measures how much you want to work within the system's bounds, through electoral politics and legal activism. Those who lean towards Reform usually view themselves as being realistic, and view violent tactics as unfeasible, unethical or otherwise undesirable.


Class Axis

Domination

measures your favouritism for, or against, certain social or economic classes. Those who lean towards Domination typically view the interests of different classes as incompatible, and wish to inhibit or even remove social classes they view as opposing the national interest.



Collaboration

measures your belief in aligning the interests of all social and economic classes towards the common good. Those who lean towards Collaboration view strife among the classes as damaging to the nation, and typically desire a class-neutral state to resolve the conflict that does exist.


Diplomatic Axis

Autarky

measures your desire for national self-sufficiency, both economically and diplomatically. Those who lean towards Autarky view dependency on other states as limits on their nation's sovereignty and would consider it ideal for their nation to retreat from global politics.



Hegemony

measures how much you want your nation to participate in the international stage. Those who lean towards Hegemony typically view forming alliances with ideologically aligned states as necessary, whether they wish to lead such geopolitical blocs or be one of its participating members.


Identity Axis

Citizenship

measures how much you put emphasis on cultural values in your conception of the nation. Those who lead towards Citizenship typically view the nation and its culture as based on core values rather than heritage, and that through hard work and service to the nation can become apart of it.



Kinship

measures how much you value ancestry, ethnicity and/or race in your conception of the nation. Those who lean towards Kinship typically view one's nation and culture as inseparable from ancestral ties, and often wish to repatriate or otherwise be separated from those they deem as foreign.


Cultural Axis

Transgressive

measures your desire to build a new social order rather than sticking to tradition. Those who lean towards Transgressive typically view many traditions as antiquated and no longer useful or even harmful to the nation, and view the cultivation of a new culture as essential to the nation's rebirth.



Restoration

measures your affinity towards past social and cultural values. Those who learn towards Restoration usually view the ills of the modern day as a result of departure from a previous social order, and view it as imperative to undo these changes.


Technological Axis

Development

measures how much you want to advance technological and industrial development. Those who lean towards Technological usually view a technological advantage and strong industrial base as key to the success of a nation, and that inhibition on growth will only harm the nation.



Preservation

measures your desire to preserve nature. Those who lean towards Preservation typically view industry and several modern technologies as detrimental to both the environment and mankind, and often believe that a key element of nationalism is caring for the nation's flora and fauna.


Political Axis

Totality

measures your belief in collectivism and ideological homogeneity. Those who lean towards Totality usually consider the toleration of opposing views as detrimental to national unity, and that political power ought to be centralised in an ideologically homogenous party, representative of the collective interest.



Plurality

measures your belief in political dialogue and distribution of power. Those who lean towards Plurality typically are wary of the nationalist movement being superseded by an ideololgically-purist state which inhibits the individual rather than uplifting him.


Religious Axis

Material

measures your affinity towards anti-clerical and anti-religious views. Those who lean towards Material often view organised religion, or even religion as a whole, as an inhibition on the nation which has no place in the modern day.



Spiritual

measures your desire for faith to influence society and policy. Those who lean towards Spiritual are typically highly religious and view faith as essential to the ethical and cultural foundation of society.
